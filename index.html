03 / PERFORMANCE⏣

Parallel Programming

Parallelized a numerical-integration workload and investigated synchronization and memory-performance behavior.

COpenMPMPI
Explore the results

Compared critical sections, atomics, locks, and reductions. Fixed false sharing with cache-line padding and thread-private partial sums. The reduction version achieved 0.063s versus 0.194s serial — a 3.1× speedup on four threads. Also demonstrated and fixed an MPI send/receive deadlock using MPI_Sendrecv.
